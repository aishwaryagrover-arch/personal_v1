APAR under MahaPAR: who does what, by when, and how

Draft for discussion with GAD | IDinsight | Based on Form A, for Group A and Group B officials, current draft

The APAR covers the financial year just ended, 1 April to 31 March, and the cycle opens on the following 1 April. The APAR for 2025-26 opens on 1 April 2026.

Every part is filed in MahaPAR and signed with a digital signature or eSign. Paper forms are not accepted.

If a stage is not completed by its date, MahaPAR forwards the APAR to the next stage, records the delay, and deducts timeliness marks from the APAR of the official who held it. These marks are 5 per cent of the final score, that is −0.5 to +0.5 on the 10-point scale, and they sit in Part 6, which MahaPAR generates on its own. Timeliness is counted for every role an official holds in the cycle: their own self-appraisal, their assessments as a reporting officer, and their reviews as a reviewing officer.

A. The cycle at a glance

31 MarchReporting year ends
1 AprilCycle opens
15 AprilOfficial confirms Part 1
30 AprilCustodian verifies Part 1 and the mapping
15 MaySelf-appraisal
15 JuneReporting officer
15 JulyReviewing officer
31 JulyAccepting authority, if GAD adds this stage and a stage is missing
31 DecemberCycle closes

Note on the dates. The dates shown are the ones proposed in the stakeholder draft, modelled on Gujarat’s cycle, which gives each stage about 30 days. They will be finalised in consultation with GAD.

After the reviewing stage the completed APAR is disclosed to the official by the custodian. The official may submit one representation within 15 days of disclosure. No entries are accepted after the cycle closes.

Maharashtra's current forms have no accepting stage. If GAD adds one, it acts only where a stage was never completed; it is not an appellate stage and does not revise grades.

D. What happens if a stage is not completed

SituationWhat happens next
Official does not confirm Part 1 by 15 AprilThe custodian completes the mapping from the service record.
Official does not submit the self-appraisal by 15 MayThe reporting officer assesses on the available record.
Reporting officer does not complete the stage by 15 JuneMahaPAR forwards the APAR and the reviewing officer completes the assessment.
Reviewing officer does not complete the stage by 15 JulyThe reporting officer's assessment becomes final.
A stage is never completed at allWhere GAD adds an accepting stage, the accepting authority records the missing stage and the action taken (Part 5).
Pendency in an office is not movingCustodian sends reminders, then the Cadre Monitoring Authority takes it up with the custodian, then the Sub Department Monitoring Authority, then GAD.

Follow-up runs upward: official, custodian, Cadre Monitoring Authority, Sub Department Monitoring Authority, GAD. Reminders to an official are sent by the custodian. Each level above follows up with the level below it, not with the official.
