APAR under MahaPAR: who does what, by when, and how

Draft for discussion with GAD · IDinsight · follows Form A (Group A and B) and Form B (Group C), current drafts · deadlines are proposals

The APAR covers the financial year just ended, 1 April to 31 March, and the cycle opens on the following 1 April. The APAR for 2025-26 opens on 1 April 2026.

Every part is filed in MahaPAR and signed with a digital signature or eSign. Paper forms are not accepted.

If a stage is not completed by its date, MahaPAR forwards the APAR to the next stage, records the delay, and deducts timeliness marks from the APAR of the official who held it. These marks are 5% of the final score, that is −0.5 to +0.5 on the 10-point scale, and they sit in Part 6, which MahaPAR generates on its own. Timeliness is counted for every role an official holds in the cycle: their own self-appraisal, their assessments as a reporting officer, and their reviews as a reviewing officer.

C. What happens if a stage is not completed

SituationWhat happens next
Official does not confirm Part 1 by 15 AprilThe custodian completes the mapping from the service record.
Official does not submit the self-appraisal by 15 MayThe reporting officer assesses on the available record.
Reporting officer does not complete the stage by 15 JuneMahaPAR forwards the APAR and the reviewing officer completes the assessment.
Reviewing officer does not complete the stage by 15 JulyThe reporting officer's assessment becomes final.
A stage is never completed at allWhere GAD adds an accepting stage, the accepting authority records the missing stage and the action taken (Part 5).
Pendency in an office is not movingCustodian sends reminders, then the Cadre Monitoring Authority takes it up with the custodian, then the Sub Department Monitoring Authority, then GAD.

Follow-up runs upward: official, custodian, Cadre Monitoring Authority, Sub Department Monitoring Authority, GAD. Reminders to an official are sent by the custodian. Each level above follows up with the level below it, not with the official.

Note on the dates. The dates shown are the ones proposed in the stakeholder draft, modelled on Gujarat's cycle, which gives each stage about 30 days. They will be finalised in consultation with GAD.

After the reviewing stage the completed APAR is disclosed to the official by the custodian. The official may submit one representation within 15 days of disclosure. No entries are accepted after the cycle closes.

E. Points for GAD to confirm

  1. The reporting chain is now mapped by the custodianThe earlier draft had the official enter the reporting and reviewing officers for the custodian to verify. Form A v4.1 places the mapping with the custodian and leaves the official a correction request. The responsibility has moved between two roles, so the resolution should say so plainly.
  2. The reviewing officer works on the overall grade onlyThe earlier draft allowed the reviewing officer to revise individual dimensions, with MahaPAR recalculating. Form A v4.1 asks only about the overall grade and states that dimension-wise grades are not revised.
  3. The accepting authority is not an appellate stageThe accepting stage is optional and Maharashtra's current forms have none. If GAD adds it, the earlier draft let it record a revised overall grade, while Form A v4.1 opens Part 5 only where the reporting or reviewing stage is missing and asks only which stage is missing and what action was taken. The second is followed here.
  4. Form references have shiftedIn Form A v4.1 grading is 3.1, integrity 3.2, the overall assessment 3.3, agreement with the self-appraisal 3.4 and training nominations 3.5. The technical cadre question is 2.6, with four modules, T1 to T4.
  5. DeadlinesThe dates above are the ones proposed in the stakeholder draft, modelled on Gujarat's cycle, which gives each stage about 30 days. The digital walkthrough currently shows 31 May for the reporting stage. One of the two needs to be corrected once GAD confirms the dates.
  6. New roles need to be nominatedThe Cadre Monitoring Authority and Sub Department Monitoring Authority points of contact do not exist today. The resolution should require each department to nominate them and intimate GAD within a stated period.
