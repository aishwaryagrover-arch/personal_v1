My dat man,

I don't have words big enough, so I'm using all the small ones. Thank you for being my best friend before you were anything else. Thank you for laughing at me, with me, sometimes instead of me.

This year tried very hard to break me. You quietly refused to let it. You made me feel held from a different country, which I didn't know was possible until you.

I can't wait for the next Goa. The next Chaayos. The next everything. Until then — happy birthday, my silly goose. The world got softer the day you were born.
